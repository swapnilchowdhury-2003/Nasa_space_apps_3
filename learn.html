Why does your body change in space?

Microgravity and the space environment change how the heart, blood, muscles and sleep work. These are the five changes CrewCheck keeps an eye on, explained in plain language. This page is education, not a diagnosis.

AWhat to expect, in order

01 · EARLY FLIGHT

Fluids shift toward the head and chest. Face feels full and legs get thinner. Sleep and body clock start to be disturbed.

02 · LONG FLIGHT

Heart, muscles and aerobic fitness slowly deteriorate, even with daily exercise. Chronic short sleep builds up.

03 · RETURN TO GRAVITY

Blood pools in the legs when standing. Dizziness and fainting risk goes up. Reconditioning takes weeks to months.
