10How CrewCheck maps to the judging criteria

Comparative baselines and metabolic benchmarks powered by NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR) Study OSD-575 (SpaceX Inspiration4), NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (LSDA), and HRP Cardiovascular Roadmap frameworks. BP analog data from published studies; Framingham is non-NASA.

Use of AI: this application was drafted with Claude (Anthropic). Decision support only; not a medical device and not a diagnosis. Escalation values must be replaced by Flight-Surgeon-approved mission protocols.
